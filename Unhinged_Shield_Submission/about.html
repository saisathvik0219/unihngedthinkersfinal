The Team

About UNHINGED THINKERS

We are a group of passionate individuals dedicated to exploring the intersection of technology and human ethics. In a world increasingly driven by automated decision-making, our project focuses on preserving digital integrity and critical thinking.

Our team name, UNHINGED THINKERS, reflects our commitment to stepping outside conventional boundaries and questioning the status quo of AI dependency.

Our Mission

To analyze the psychological impact of AI over-reliance and propose ethical frameworks that empower users to maintain their cognitive independence.
