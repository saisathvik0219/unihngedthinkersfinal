Prioritization Matrix

We've mapped our top 30 solutions onto a Value vs. Effort Matrix to determine where to focus our energy.

How to read this graph:

This graph uses a 10x10 number plane to evaluate ideas. The X-axis (Horizontal) represents the Effort required to build the solution (1 = Easy, 10 = Complex). The Y-axis (Vertical) represents the Value or impact of the solution (1 = Low, 10 = High Impact).

  • 🟢 Quick Wins: High Impact, Low Effort (Build these first!)
  • 🟠 Major Projects: High Impact, High Effort (Strategic long-term goals)
  • 🔵 Fill-ins: Low Impact, Low Effort (Do if there's spare time)
  • 🔴 Low Priority: Low Impact, High Effort (Avoid these)
