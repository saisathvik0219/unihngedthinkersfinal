The Proposal

Unhinged Shield

A multi-agent browser integrity layer that combats blind AI trust through real-time factual auditing and privacy protection.

The Vision

The Unhinged Shield is a browser extension designed to protect users from the two greatest risks of modern AI: Hallucinations and Data Leaks. By sitting between the user and the AI, it introduces a necessary moment of reflection.

The Evolution

Convergence of Concepts

The Unhinged Shield is the final synthesis of three high-performing ideas from our 6-3-5 brainwriting phase.

Compare AIs to find the truth

"We should make a system that asks three different AI models the same question and compares their answers to see if they are lying."

Add a blur to make us pause

"I think it should blur out the AI response if it's suspicious, so that we have to click a button and actually think before we read it."

Block secret info before it's sent

"Let's build a plugin that scans for passwords or private info while you type and blocks the connection before the AI can even see it."
