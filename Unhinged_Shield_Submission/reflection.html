Phase 10

Reflection

A summary of our design thinking process and the key takeaways from the Unhinged Thinkers project.

Project Summary

Our project focused on the growing issue of Blind AI Trust. We started by exploring why people accept AI answers without question and used research tools like the 5-Whys to find the root causes. We discovered that a combination of mental laziness and a desire for instant answers was creating a safety risk for users.

To solve this, we developed the Unhinged Shield prototype—a browser extension that helps users double-check facts and protects their private data from being leaked to AI models.

Useful Takeaways

User Awareness

We realized that many users are completely unaware of AI hallucinations and data privacy risks.

Safety by Design

Safety tools should be built into the workflow, like our "Stop-and-Confirm" privacy guard.

Critical Thinking

The goal of our solution is to encourage users to pause and think rather than just copy-pasting.

Collaborative Work

Working as a team helped us explore hundreds of ideas and select the most feasible solution.
